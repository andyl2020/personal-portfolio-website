What this is not

  • It's not a set of lines to memorize. You build real skills by doing real reps.
  • It doesn't promise phone numbers, dates, or any specific result from another person.
  • It's not therapy. If you're dealing with something a mental health professional should help with, please talk to one.

About me

I'm Andy Luu, a coach based in Vancouver, BC.

In high school I was very introverted. I couldn't hold eye contact, and I couldn't keep a conversation going. Social skills didn't come naturally to me, so I learned them from the ground up, one uncomfortable conversation at a time.

That's why I coach. I remember what it's like to want to say something and walk away without saying it. I'm currently working toward my ACC coaching certification.

You can also find me on YouTube.

Questions

I'm really introverted. Is this for me?

Yes. That's exactly where I started. We go at a pace you can actually keep, and build from small wins.

Will this get me dates?

I can't promise what another person will do, so I don't sell that. What I help with is your side: confidence, starting conversations, and handling what comes up. I'm glad to give feedback on dating situations along the way.

Do I need to live in Vancouver?

No. Coaching is by video call and written feedback, so it works wherever you live.
