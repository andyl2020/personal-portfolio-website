Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Andy Luu Coaching ("I", "me") is an independent coaching practice run by Andy Luu in British Columbia, Canada. This policy explains what personal information I collect, how I use it, who I share it with, and how I protect it.

What I collect

  • Contact details you give me: your name, email, and phone number.
  • Coaching information: what you share in calls, messages, and field reports, and my notes and feedback.
  • Call recordings and transcripts, only if you agree to a session being recorded.
  • Payment details: payments are processed by Stripe or PayPal, or sent by e-Transfer. I don't see or store your full card number.

How I use it

  • To deliver coaching, prepare for sessions, and give you feedback.
  • To schedule sessions, send receipts, and communicate with you.
  • To keep business and tax records.

I don't sell your information, and I don't share your coaching details or use them in marketing without your written permission.

Recordings and transcripts

I only record a session when you agree to it. I may use transcription software, including AI transcription tools, to turn a recording into notes. Recordings and transcripts are kept private, used only for your coaching, and deleted when they're no longer needed. You can ask me not to record at any time.

Who it's shared with

I share information only with service providers I use to run the practice, and only what they need: payment processors (Stripe, PayPal), video call, email, calendar, and note-taking or transcription tools. These providers may store data outside Canada. I'll also disclose information if the law requires it.

How I protect it

I keep your information in password-protected accounts with two-factor authentication where available, limit access to myself, and delete what I no longer need.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the personal information I hold about you by emailing me. I'll respond within 30 days. Some records, like payment receipts, may need to be kept for tax purposes.
